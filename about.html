ABOUT HEKSAA

Technology designed around timely human support.

HEKSAA is a prototype concept for AI-powered dynamic mental health monitoring and distress prediction for victims of atrocities.

Purpose

Support regular well-being check-ins, surface concerning patterns and help professionals respond earlier.

How it works

Patients provide self-reported check-in information. The prototype converts those inputs into simple indicators for demonstration.

Responsible AI

Prototype indicators are not clinically validated and must not replace qualified assessment, diagnosis or emergency care.
