PRIVACY

Your information deserves careful handling.

This fresh frontend prototype stores demonstration profile and check-in data in the browser's localStorage. It is not a production healthcare system.

Local prototype storage

Until a backend is connected, data stays in the browser used for the prototype and is not synchronized across devices.

Future architecture

A production implementation should use authenticated access, appropriate encryption, access controls, audit logging and validated privacy practices.

Important limitation

HEKSAA prototype risk indicators are not a medical diagnosis and should not be used alone for clinical decisions.
